Welcome to the Forest Ecology Lab

Led by Prof. Sisira Ediriweera, our lab maintains long-term forest census plots across Sri Lanka's elevation gradient, tracking individually tagged trees through repeated census intervals to understand how these forests are changing over time.

We ask how tree survival, growth and recruitment shape forest structure, diversity and carbon storage, and how these processes respond to environmental change.

Research

Field sites and research themes

Lowland rainforest

Sinharaja Forest Reserve

A UNESCO World Heritage lowland tropical rainforest and one of the last extensive stands of primary forest in Sri Lanka, with a permanent plot for long-term census work.

Montane forest

Horton Plains National Park

A high-elevation cloud forest and grassland mosaic in the central highlands, used for comparative work on demography and biomass across an elevation gradient.
